OPERATIONAL PROCESS

The Field Sampling Workflow

01

Producer Request

Producer submits lot details, harvest window, location, and producer license numbers via our secure intake portal.

02

Verification & Schedule

Agent verifies active producer registration, confirms lot acreage/strain, and schedules an official sampling window.

03

Field Sampling

Sampling agent arrives on site, inspects lot boundaries, collects representative floral cuts, and records GPS coordinates.

04

Chain of Custody & Lab Handoff

Sample is sealed in tamper-evident security bags, documented with Chain of Custody forms, and dispatched to the designated testing lab.

# DSC USDA — Hemp Sampling & Compliance Field Services

Public website repository for **DSC USDA** — providing compliant hemp field sampling services, rigorous chain-of-custody protocols, and professional sampling agent workflows under the Dark Star Ecosystem.

## Public Brand & Disclaimer Rules
- **Independent Operations**: DSC USDA is a private sampling service division within the Dark Star ecosystem and is **NOT the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA)**.
- **Verified Credentials**: Sampling agents have completed official USDA Hemp Sampling Training. Official agent certificate details will be posted upon final administrative issuance.
- **Service Boundaries**: DSC USDA does not determine final regulatory compliance, guarantee laboratory test outcomes, or act as an accredited testing laboratory.

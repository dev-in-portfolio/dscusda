FIELD READY CHECKLIST

Producer Preparation Guidelines

Pre-Appointment Checklist

  • ✔ Document Readiness: Ensure state/USDA producer license certificates, lot field maps, and DEA lab submission forms are accessible on site.
  • ✔ Designated Representative: An authorized producer representative must be present to escort the sampling agent across designated lot boundaries.
  • ✔ Clear Field Access: Ensure field rows, greenhouse aisles, and facility entrances are free of hazardous obstructions and accessible during the scheduled window.
  • ✔ Lot Demarcation: Verify that lot boundaries match submitted field maps and registered lot identifier numbers.

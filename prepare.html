FIELD PREPARATION

Pre-Sampling Producer Checklist

Ensure your facility or field lot is prepared for efficient compliance collection prior to the agent's arrival.

ON-SITE CHECKLIST

Have Ready On-Site

  • Current Producer License & Registration Documentation
  • Designated Lot Identification & Field Boundary Map
  • Clear Physical Access to All Designated Field or Greenhouse Lots
  • Authorized Producer Representative Present During Sampling Visit
PRE-VISIT LOGISTICS

Confirm Prior to Visit

  • Anticipated Harvest Schedule (Must Fall Within Regulatory Window)
  • Designated Analytical Testing Laboratory Selection
  • Total Number of Separate Lots Requiring Individual Sampling
  • Site Entry Instructions, Gate Codes, or Biosecurity Protocols

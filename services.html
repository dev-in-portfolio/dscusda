FIELD SPECIFICATIONS

Sampling Services Overview

JURISDICTIONAL STATEMENT: Services are offered strictly within verified operating territories and subject to state/federal producer licensing validation. DSC USDA does not make unsupported jurisdictional claims.

Commercial Field Pre-Harvest Sampling

On-site collection of representative floral samples from designated commercial lots. Samples are taken from the top third of female plants following standard saw-pattern or grid-pattern collection methods.

Greenhouse & Indoor Facility Sampling

Controlled environmental sampling tailored for indoor benches, vertical racks, and greenhouse bays. Ensures accurate lot demarcation for multi-harvest cycles.

Chain of Custody & Lab Dispatch

Complete physical sample prep, moisture control packaging, tamper-evident security sealing, and shipment to your designated DEA-registered analytical laboratory.
